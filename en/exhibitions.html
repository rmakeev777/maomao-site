solo exhibition

Between Moments

Teaory Teahouse, Bangkok, Thailand

My first solo exhibition, dedicated to the beauty of fleeting moments and the ability to notice them in everyday life. It brought together digital illustrations and paintings inspired by life in Thailand, nature and quiet human stories.

2026

art performance

Inner Garden

Yoga Shala, Bangkok, Thailand

A joint performance with a Thai artist that combined painting, meditation and interaction with the audience. The project explored how a person’s inner state connects with the creative process and nature, turning the making of an artwork into a shared experience.

For me it matters to create not just drawing classes, but a community of people brought together by a love of art.
