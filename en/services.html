How it works

  1. 1

    Discussing the project

    We discuss the idea, goals, format, references and your wishes. At this stage we agree on timing and price.

  2. 2

    Concept and sketch

    If needed, I make one or more sketches so we can agree on the overall composition and mood of the piece.

  3. 3

    Creating the work

    Once the concept is approved, I start working. For longer projects I can share work-in-progress stages.

  4. 4

    Final approval

    The finished work goes through a final review. Small adjustments are made if the project allows for them.

  5. 5

    Payment and delivery

    When the project is complete, the final payment is made. The work is handed over in person or shipped in secure packaging by the method of your choice.

  6. Please note

    A 50% deposit confirms the order. It reserves a place in my schedule so I can start working on your project.
