Privacy policy

Version of 6 October 2026. This is a courtesy translation; the Russian version prevails.

1. General provisions

1.1. This Policy sets out how the personal data of users of the website of the artist Katerina Makeeva (the “Website”) are processed and protected.

1.2. The personal data controller is Katerina Makeeva, registered as self-employed under the Russian “Professional Income Tax” regime, Tax ID (INN) [INN] (the “Controller”). Contact email: kate.maomao@mail.ru.

1.3. This Policy has been prepared in accordance with Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data”.

2. What data are processed

Through the contact form a user may provide: their name; contact details (phone, email address, messenger or social media username); phone number; a description of their idea or request. The Website does not collect special categories of personal data.

3. Purposes of processing

Personal data are processed solely to respond to the user’s enquiry, to discuss and fulfil an order (a painting, illustration, design, participation in an art meetup or workshop), and to communicate about purchasing works.

4. Legal grounds

Processing is based on the user’s consent, given by ticking the corresponding box in the form and submitting a request, and on the performance of a contract to which the user is a party.

5. Processing procedure and retention

5.1. Form data are sent to the Controller by email using the Web3Forms form delivery service.

5.2. Data are kept no longer than the purposes of processing require and no more than 3 years from the last contact, unless otherwise required by law.

5.3. The Controller does not transfer personal data to third parties, except as required by the laws of the Russian Federation.

6. User rights

Users may request information about the processing of their data, ask for it to be corrected, blocked or deleted, and withdraw their consent by writing to kate.maomao@mail.ru. The Controller will respond within the period set by law.

7. Cookies and third-party services

The Website does not use advertising trackers or analytics. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which may receive technical request data (IP address, browser type). Your language choice is stored in your browser (localStorage) and is not sent anywhere.

8. Final provisions

The Controller may amend this Policy. The current version is always available on this page.
